Exploring Jupiter

Jupiter, the largest planet in our solar system, is a gas giant composed mostly of hydrogen and helium. Known for its colorful cloud bands and powerful storms, it has a mass more than 300 times that of Earth.

The Great Red Spot

A colossal storm larger than Earth that has been raging for centuries. Its reddish hue comes from complex organic molecules, red phosphorus, or other elements interacting in Jupiter’s atmosphere.

Jupiter’s Moons

Jupiter has 79 known moons, with the four largest being the Galilean moons: Io, Europa, Ganymede, and Callisto. Learn more from NASA.

Atmosphere and Magnetic Field

Jupiter’s atmosphere is composed mainly of hydrogen and helium, with traces of ammonia, methane, and water vapor. Its magnetic field is 20,000 times stronger than Earth’s, producing intense radiation belts.
